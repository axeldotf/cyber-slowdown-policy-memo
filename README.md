# Policy Memo — Mitigating the Strategic Impact of the Cyber Slowdown

Policy memo written for the **Technology and Change in Global Politics** exam
(University of Bologna).

## Abstract
This memo analyzes the *cyber slowdown* — the phase in which digital innovation
keeps advancing, but its positive impact is increasingly undermined by systemic
threats: cyberattacks, algorithmic polarization, disinformation, and harm to mental
wellbeing. Addressed to the leadership of the GAFAM companies (Google, Apple, Meta,
Amazon, Microsoft), it proposes six areas of coordinated action to rebuild trust,
defend cognitive rights, and align innovation with democratic values.

## Contents
- Executive summary and problem statement
- Risk Prioritization Matrix
- Scenario analysis (HILP framework)
- 6 policy recommendations (algorithm transparency, fact-checking, democratic
  safeguards, ethical mobilization, digital wellbeing, shared governance)
- Implementation plan with timeline
- Glossary of key terms
